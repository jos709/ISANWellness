How to Prepare GentleDaily

Enjoying GentleDaily as part of your daily routine is simple.

  1. Measure: Add 5 grams (1 serving) of GentleDaily Original or Golden to a glass containing approximately 300 mL of drinking water.
  2. Mix: Stir thoroughly until the powder is evenly dispersed throughout the water.
  3. Drink: Consume immediately after mixing, before the mixture begins to thicken.
  4. Daily use: Start with 1 serving per day and drink sufficient water throughout the day.

Important: Always mix the powder with an adequate amount of water. Never consume the dry powder directly without mixing it with water.
